# Filter and Specialty Blend Archetypes

## Pour-Over / Drip Blends

Filter brewing extracts coffee at lower temperature (88–96°C) and without pressure. This makes acidity more pleasant and body less important than in espresso. Good filter blends are designed to be transparent and complex.

### Filter Archetype A: East African Floral

**Goal**: Jasmine, peach, lemon curd, clean finish.
**Components**:
- 55–65% Ethiopia Yirgacheffe washed — floral, citrus, tea
- 35–45% Rwanda or Burundi washed — stone fruit, cleanness
**Roast**: Light.
**Profile**: Acidity 7.5–8.5, Body 3.5–5.0, Sweetness 7.0–8.5.

### Filter Archetype B: Berry and Chocolate

**Goal**: Blueberry, dark chocolate, caramel.
**Components**:
- 40–50% Ethiopia Sidama natural — blueberry, wine
- 30–40% Colombia washed — balance, clean chocolate
- 15–20% Guatemala honey — sweetness, caramel
**Roast**: Medium-Light.
**Profile**: Acidity 6.0–7.5, Body 5.5–7.0, Sweetness 7.5–8.5.

## Milk-Based Espresso Blends

### Flat White / Cappuccino Blend

The challenge: acidity must be low enough that it does not turn sour with steamed milk. Body must be high enough to not get lost in the dairy.

**Goal**: Caramel, milk chocolate, brown sugar. Silky texture.
**Components**:
- 50–60% Brazil natural — base, chocolate, body
- 25–35% Colombia washed — smooth balance
- 10–20% Costa Rica honey — sweetness, clean finish
**Roast**: Medium.
**Profile**: Acidity 3.5–5.5, Body 7.0–8.5, Sweetness 7.5–9.0.

### Oat Milk Pairing Blend

Oat milk adds its own sweetness and a slightly grainy, creamy texture. It pairs best with chocolate-forward blends.

**Goal**: Dark chocolate, brown sugar, faint caramel.
**Components**:
- 60–70% Brazil medium-dark — body, dark chocolate
- 20–30% Sulawesi Toraja — spice, body, complexity
- 5–15% Ethiopia natural (at ≤15%) — slight fruit lift without acidity clash
**Roast**: Medium-Dark.
**Profile**: Acidity 2.5–4.5, Body 8.0–9.5, Sweetness 5.5–7.0.

## Seasonal / Limited Blends

### Holiday Spice Blend

**Goal**: Dark chocolate, brown spice, Christmas cake, cozy.
**Components**:
- 50% Brazil natural
- 25% Sumatra Mandheling wet-hulled — earthy, tobacco, cedar
- 15% Sulawesi Toraja — dark chocolate, dried fruit
- 10% Colombia medium-dark — balance
**Roast**: Medium-Dark.

### Summer Cold Brew

**Goal**: Clean, refreshing, low acidity, chocolate and caramel.
**Components**:
- 60% Brazil natural
- 25% Sumatra Gayo Aceh
- 15% Costa Rica honey
**Roast**: Medium.
**Brew**: 1:7 ratio cold brew, 18 hours at 4°C.
