# Blend Naming and Description Guidelines

When the AI generates a blend name and description, it should follow these principles to produce copy that reads like a person wrote it.

## Blend Names

**Good blend names**:
- Evocative of the flavor profile or brewing context: "Graveyard Shift", "Sunday Slow", "Watershed", "Red Earth", "Morning Light"
- Inspired by the dominant origin or farmer story: "Sidama Rise", "Gayo Valley", "Santos Gold"
- Simple and memorable: 2–4 words maximum
- Not marketing clichés: avoid "Premium Blend", "Excellence", "Gold Reserve", "Elite"

**Bad blend names** (do not use):
- Generic: "Bold Espresso Blend", "Coffee Blend No. 1"
- Over-the-top: "God Tier Coffee", "Ultimate Supreme Blend"
- Ingredient-listing: "Brazil Colombia Ethiopia Blend"
- AI tells: "AI Generated Blend", "Optimal Flavor Profile"

**Naming strategy**: Let the dominant component, the flavor target, or the intended moment of consumption inspire the name. A heavy, earthy Indonesian blend might be "Deep Soil". A bright Ethiopian-Kenya filter blend might be "High Wire".

---

## Descriptions

**Length**: 2–4 sentences. 50–200 words.

**Tone**: Direct, sensory, honest. Write like a knowledgeable barista describing a cup — not a marketing document.

**Structure**:
1. The flavor experience (what you taste, in order: first impression, mid-palate, finish)
2. The intent or use case (best for espresso, works in milk, great cold brew base)
3. Optional: a note about the dominant origin or producer story

**Good description example**:
"A bold, grounding espresso with dark chocolate on the front, molasses sweetness in the mid-palate, and a long, cedar-spiced finish. The heavy body holds through milk — it was designed for flat whites and lattes. The base comes from Sumatran wet-hulled beans farmed at Gayo Aceh, which give it its characteristic weight and low acidity."

**Bad description example** (do not produce):
"This premium AI-generated coffee blend features an optimal balance of carefully selected beans from multiple origins to deliver a superior taste experience with enhanced flavor complexity."

## Reasoning Text

The reasoning field explains why specific beans were chosen and in what percentages.

**Good reasoning**:
"Brazil Santos at 55% provides the chocolate and hazelnut base with enough body to hold the blend together in milk. The Gayo Aceh at 25% adds weight and low-key earthiness without overwhelming the sweetness. Ethiopia Yirgacheffe at 20% brings a citrus lift that stops the blend from reading as flat — at this percentage it brightens without making the shot sour in milk."

**Bad reasoning**:
"The AI selected these beans because they have the best flavor profile for your request and the percentages were optimized to achieve the desired characteristics."

**Rules**:
- Name each bean and explain why it was chosen at its specific percentage.
- Acknowledge the flavor contribution and why the percentage is set where it is.
- Do not use phrases like "AI selected", "algorithmically determined", "optimized".
- Do not describe what the owner asked for — describe what the blend delivers.
